My Story

I come from a small village in Telangana. From Sri Chaitanya Vidyalayam to intermediate in Khammam to a Computer Science degree in Chennai, every stage of my education meant stepping into a bigger arena — and earning my place in it. That journey shaped the two strengths I bring to every team: I adapt quickly, and I learn faster than the situation demands.

At AsInt, I engineer the Asset Intelligence Suite — an enterprise platform that safeguards critical infrastructure in the oil & gas industry, where reliability is non-negotiable. I build across the full depth of the stack: Java service logic, CDS data models, OData V4 APIs, and performance-tuned SAP HANA Cloud queries on Cloud Foundry. When critical production issues threatened client operations, I drove the root-cause analysis and resolution — work recognized with a formal Client Appreciation Award for preventing business disruption.

Beyond delivery, I operate as a force multiplier: mentoring colleagues on Java and SAP CAP architecture — recognized with the company's Coach Badge — and serving as the bridge between clients and engineering: clarifying requirements, documenting API contracts, and delivering on commitment, sprint after sprint.

I'm now extending that foundation into Google Cloud Platform, Kubernetes, Docker, PostgreSQL, RabbitMQ and Spring Batch — with a clear ambition: delivering complete products, end to end — architecture, data, APIs, and a deployed system that runs reliably in production. Give me a problem worth solving, and I'll bring the energy, ownership and standards it deserves.
